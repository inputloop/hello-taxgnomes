#+TITLE: hello-taxgnomes: the Gnemonic Word Mines
#+AUTHOR: Corwin Brust and Contributors

A reading component built from a taxonomy at build time.  Pick a word from the word game's four
tables; move a four-way slider: north simpler, south denser, east moodier, west drier; the centre
is the plain gloss.  Every sentence says who wrote it: a dictionary, the game's pooled sentences, a
template, or a local model.  A worked example for egad: its egad and egadjit hooks are suggestions,
and the page works alone.

#+begin_example
make            # unfold the recipe, build the readings, write build/hello-taxgnomes.html
make check      # both unfolders agree; the build is honest; the second reader agrees; prove-red
python3 tools/tg1dr.py ingest MODEL ANSWER.json   # take in sentences a local model drafted
#+end_example

The builder reads the word game (=../mini-c4-game=, or =TG_GAME=) and its caches of WordNet 3.1 and
GCIDE 0.53, and SCOWL's word list as hunspell ships it.  Everything is unfolded from
=hello-taxgnomes.org=; edit that file.  Built under build-loop: the design, the QA plan and the
notes of each pass are in =.house/h/=.
